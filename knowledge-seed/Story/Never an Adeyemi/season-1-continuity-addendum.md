# Never an Adeyemi — Season 1 continuity addendum

This addendum records the concrete choices made while drafting Episodes 2–5. It supplements Mario's story bible; it does not replace it.

## Chosen details

- The old family legal handler is Emmanuel Afolabi, the lawyer who handled the first company records.
- The original forgery was a replacement deed with a copied signature, filed after the equal partnership was established.
- Chief's conclusive private evidence was an unsent letter to Mr. Obi, kept with the replacement deed in a locked box in Chief's study. Mrs. Adeyemi finds the box while preparing the memorial and gives it to Kunle and Yewande to protect the family. They burn the letter and destroy the deed.
- Afolabi kept a draft of an admission letter and can testify about what he saw. His material corroborates Mr. Obi's original partnership agreement and testimony; it does not make the historical forgery a criminal case.
- Episode 2 ends with Ify discovering the childhood photograph of Mr. Obi and Chief. The discovery changes her understanding of the family and creates the paid-continuation question: what happened after the boys grew up?
- Episode 4's public failure is fixed: Yewande attacks Mr. Obi, Ify defends him, Tobi stays silent, Ify rejects Tobi, and Yewande's "spoilt brat and coward" line is the final beat.
- Episode 5's police action concerns the present-day waiver/coercion and destruction of evidence. The historic ownership wrong is honoured through the will, the civil transfer, testimony, and moral reckoning.
