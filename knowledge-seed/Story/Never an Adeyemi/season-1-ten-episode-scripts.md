These are the revised ten-minute episode drafts. They preserve the approved plot, characters and ending while dividing the former five-episode story into ten shorter chapters.

## Production note

Each draft is written for approximately ten minutes after performance, pauses, reactions, transitions and suspense holds are added in the edit. Before generation, the production agent should create a scene timing sheet and trim or expand dialogue against the recorded voices.
