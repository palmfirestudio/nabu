# Palmfire ten-episode pilot series

## Locked format

- **Episodes:** 10
- **Runtime:** approximately 10 minutes per episode
- **Season runtime:** approximately 100 minutes
- **Release plan:** one episode at a time
- **Primary language:** English at launch
- **Master format:** 16:9, 720p for the first release; 1080p when the workflow is stable
- **Discovery:** YouTube, Facebook and short-form excerpts
- **Paid access:** Palmfire website, one-time Paystack checkout

## Important production constraint

The creative pilot is ten short episodes. The $100 monthly budget means the season must be produced sequentially, with limited animation and reusable assets. The first deliverable is a complete episode-one package and a short visual test, not ten fully rendered episodes at once.

### Free-to-paid release gate

Episodes 1 and 2 are the free release. Episode 2 ends when Ify finds the childhood photograph of Obinna and Chief together. The discovery changes her understanding of her father and creates the urgent question that drives the paid continuation: what happened after the boys grew up? It does not reveal the full ownership history.

## Episode timing model

Each episode uses four sequences of approximately two to three minutes:

1. Hook and immediate situation
2. Objective and first obstacle
3. Escalation and reversal
4. Crisis, cliffhanger and next-episode movement

Each sequence contains one to three scenes. Scenes are normally forty seconds to two minutes. Individual generated shots are usually four to ten seconds, but the edit may hold still images longer when performance, narration or suspense carries the moment.

## Episode deliverables

For every episode, create:

- approved outline;
- screenplay;
- continuity report;
- sequence and scene map;
- character and location references;
- visual shot packets;
- dialogue and voice files;
- music and sound log;
- captions or transcript;
- final video master;
- poster and thumbnail;
- YouTube trailer or excerpt;
- website description and access setting;
- rights and disclosure log.

## Release gate

Do not publish an episode until a human confirms:

- the story is understandable;
- the ending works;
- the visuals are consistent enough;
- the audio is intelligible;
- the episode is correctly captioned;
- the title and thumbnail are not misleading;
- all inputs and model plans have been logged;
- the paid-access promise matches what the viewer receives.
