# Palmfire ethos

## Purpose

Palmfire exists to make original stories that are affordable to produce, easy to discover and emotionally worth returning to.

AI is Palmfire’s production instrument. It is not the identity of the company, the substitute for taste or the excuse for careless work.

## What Palmfire believes

### Human direction comes first

Every story begins with a human decision about what the story means, who it is about, where it happens, how it begins and how it ends. Agents may develop the material, but they do not silently replace the authorial decision.

### Cheap does not mean careless

Palmfire will control cost through reuse, clear templates, limited animation and disciplined production. It will not control cost by publishing incoherent plots, broken characters, unreadable dialogue or unfinished scenes.

### Story before spectacle

Visual novelty is useful only when it supports character, conflict, emotion or suspense. A simple image with a clear dramatic purpose is more valuable than an expensive shot with no reason to exist.

### Coherence is a product feature

Characters, locations, tone, rules and consequences must remain stable. Each project needs a story bible and a visual bible before large-scale generation begins.

### Local specificity can travel

Palmfire may begin with Nigerian settings, voices, folklore, humour and social realities. It should make those details understandable without sanding away what makes them specific.

### Respect the audience

Palmfire will not disguise unfinished experiments as finished films, inflate view counts, mislead viewers with fake news or use manipulative payment language. The audience should know what is free, what is paid and what kind of experience they are buying.

### Transparency without apology

Where platform rules or audience expectations require it, Palmfire will identify synthetic or AI-assisted material. The company can be proud of its method while still being honest about it.

### Rights matter

Palmfire will keep a rights log for scripts, voices, music, reference images, stock material and model plans. It will not copy living artists, copyrighted characters or real people’s likenesses without permission.

### Human review remains mandatory

No agent may approve a final script, publish a film, spend advertising money or make a public claim without a human sign-off appropriate to the decision.

## Palmfire quality bar

Before release, every project must have:

- a clear premise and audience;
- characters with wants, obstacles and changes;
- a beginning, escalation and ending;
- understandable dialogue and sound;
- consistent names, locations and visual rules;
- captions or a transcript where practical;
- a rights and safety check;
- a clear description of what viewers receive;
- a human release decision.

## What Palmfire will not become

- a factory for unedited AI output;
- a channel built around fake events or impersonated public figures;
- a company that hides recurring charges;
- a business that spends more on generation than the audience can support;
- a brand whose name is trapped inside one model or one genre;
- a system that lets an agent publish autonomously without review.

## Audience promise

Palmfire promises short, accessible stories with a clear point of view, a reason to continue watching and enough care that the audience’s time feels respected.

## Decision rule

When a decision is unclear, choose the option that best protects:

1. story clarity;
2. audience trust;
3. rights and safety;
4. production sustainability;
5. the ability to learn from a small experiment.
